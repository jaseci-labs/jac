- **Feature: Native `datetime`, `calendar`, and `zoneinfo`**: The bundled native standard library gains a full port of CPython's `datetime` (`timedelta`, `date`, `time`, `datetime`, `tzinfo`, `timezone` with ISO/`strptime`/`strftime` and aware arithmetic over a libc `struct tm` FFI floor), `calendar` (`Calendar`/`TextCalendar`, month/year grids and formatting), and `zoneinfo` (TZif v1/v2+ parser, POSIX-footer transition rules, `ZoneInfo` with cache and `TZPATH` discovery). `datetime.astimezone` against `ZoneInfo` keys converts through real transition data end to end.
