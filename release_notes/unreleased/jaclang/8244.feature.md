- **Feature: A constructor owns its own native capability violations**: A Python-only construct inside an `init` or `postinit` no longer costs the whole module its native lowering. A constructor is now a demotion owner like any other callable: it becomes a per-class seam recorded in the coverage with a reason naming the blocking construct, shipped as an abort stub when sealed and waiver-eligible through `seal_demotion_report`, while its class layout and every sibling method still lower. Native construction of such a class refuses with a structured E5092 naming the demoted initializer instead of quietly skipping it, and the calling method cascades into a seam of its own; constructors are lowered ahead of every other body so that refusal does not depend on emission order. Constructs with no owning callable, such as a glob initializer or a field type, stay module-fatal.
