- **Serve: a walker or function reads its request and chooses its status**: `current_request()` from `jaclang.server.exchange` returns the method, path, query, headers (credentials left out) and the resolved origin of the request being served, or `None` outside a request, and `link()` builds absolute URLs from `JAC_SERVE_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` when set. `respond(status=..., headers=...)` sets the HTTP status and headers of a walker or enveloped function, so refusals answer 401, 403, 404 or 409 instead of 200 or 500; writes stay committed unless the unit raises.
