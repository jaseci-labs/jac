- **Fix: routes are matched most specific first, and a walker's implicit node route yields to declared ones**: A catch-all behind a path parameter (`/api/videos/{id}/file/{path:path}`) was ranked after the page fallback `/{file_path:path}` and answered 404 on every GET; catch-alls now rank by their fixed prefix. And every routed walker's implicit `<path>/{nd}` route tied with a declared route one segment deeper, winning when the declared parameter's name sorted after `nd`, so `PATCH /api/videos/{id}/scenes/{sceneId}` ran the wrong walker and failed with "badly formed hexadecimal UUID string"; the implicit spawn-on-node routes now always lose that tie.
