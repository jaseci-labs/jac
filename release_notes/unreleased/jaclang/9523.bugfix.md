- **Fix: typed dicts crossing the `any` boundary now carry JacVal vals**: Boxing a `dict[K, V]` into `any` used to store the dict pointer unchanged, but every `any` consumer -- a subscript on an `any` receiver, the ctypes marshalling boundary -- reads the vals array as JacVal, so each value decoded as garbage or `None`. Unboxing back to a concrete dict had the symmetric hole and could crash dereferencing JacVal bits as raw pointers. Dicts are now copied into their `<key>:jacval` flavour on the way in and rebuilt on the way out, and a bare `dict` narrow no longer retargets the payload at the erased `i64:i64` layout.
