- **Native: tuple keys in sets and dicts hash and compare their str members by content.** A `set[tuple[str, str]]` hashed the tuple's raw struct bytes and compared candidates with `memcmp`, so two tuples holding equal strings from different allocations never matched. With `ASTValidationPass` executing natively in the sealed kit, its `(name, accessor_kind)` dedup key never hit `seen_accessors` and a duplicated `@prop.setter` lost its E0076. Tuple keys are now hashed and compared field by field (str by content, scalars by value, other pointers by identity), and the tuple spec names its field signature (`tuple16_ss`, `tuple16_si64`, ...) so two tuple shapes of equal size stop sharing one helper set.
