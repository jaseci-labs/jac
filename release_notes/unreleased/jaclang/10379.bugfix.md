- **An app's impl annexes follow its lint policy**: `[apps.<name>.check.lint]` only applied to the app's entry file, so once `impls-in-annex` moved the entry's impls into `impl/<entry>.impl.jac`, the annex fell back to the workspace policy. A `cli` app that allows `print()` then failed `jac fmt --lintfix` with E3012, and the move was left undone. An annex is now linted under its module's app policy.
