- **Fix: native `bytes.decode` honours `encoding` and `errors`**: `data.decode("utf-8", errors="replace")` and the other positional and keyword forms now decode natively as CPython does. Supported codecs are utf-8, latin-1 and ascii, under their CPython aliases. Supported error handlers are `strict`, `ignore`, `replace`, `backslashreplace`, `surrogateescape` and `surrogatepass`. Strict failures raise `UnicodeDecodeError` with CPython's message. Previously the native decode copied the bytes and ignored both arguments, so invalid input was never replaced, dropped or reported.
