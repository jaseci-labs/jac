- **Fix: a walker response no longer repeats its reports inside `result`**: the executed walker in `data.result` carried a `reports` copy of `data.reports`, so every list response shipped twice, and a walker that reported itself recursed. In `api_mode` the serializer now leaves a walker's report list out of its attributes; the walker's `has` fields, `_jac_type`, `_jac_id` and `_jac_archetype` are unchanged. The embedded gateway's walker proxy, which read reports out of that copy, forwards the provider's `{result, reports}` payload instead of wrapping the walker under `result`. `sv_client.hydrate_walker_envelope` and `sv_client.function_result` are the one pair of envelope decoders the core runtime and jac-scale share (the scale RPC layer's private copies are gone), and `BridgeError` is now a `RuntimeError`, so `except RuntimeError` handlers around bridged calls keep working.
