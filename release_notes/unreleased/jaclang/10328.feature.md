- **`jac --help --all` lists hidden commands**: the top-level help hides internal verbs (`browse`, `precompile`, `native`, `dot`, `completions`, `precommit`, `gen_parser`) to keep the visible surface within the verb budget. Passing `--all` alongside `--help` (or `-h`) now adds them in a trailing `Hidden:` section, and the help screen's Options list documents the flag.
