- **Fix: the read-only Ops Console Role can read KEDA's own resources**: the Role jac-scale installs covered core, `apps`, `autoscaling`, `networking.k8s.io` and `policy` but nothing on `keda.sh`, so reading a `ScaledObject` from inside a deployed pod returned 403, and `admin/k8s_ops.jac` had no `CustomObjectsApi` or `AutoscalingV2Api` accessor to read one with even where the rule existed. The Role now grants `get`, `list` and `watch` on `scaledobjects` and `triggerauthentications`, and `custom_objects_api()` and `autoscaling_api()` join the existing accessors, answering `None` off-cluster like `core_api()` rather than raising. `watch` is granted now rather than later because adding it afterwards would mean a second RBAC change and a redeploy of every app. Prerequisite for #7405.
