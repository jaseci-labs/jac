- **Fix: native suffix string slices stored in containers are strings of their own**: under the managed and rc profiles a suffix slice (`s[k:]`) passed to `list.append`/`insert`, index assignment, a list literal, `set.add`, `dict.setdefault`, a tuple or a builtin was stored as a pointer into the middle of `s`. The cycle collector then treated that pointer as an object and wrote into the bytes before it -- a SIGBUS for a slice of a string literal, silent heap corruption otherwise. A view now reaches such consumers as its whole root or as a copy, and the copies and the view's root reference are released instead of leaked.
