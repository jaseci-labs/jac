- **Compiler infrastructure has one typed schedule and IR schema**: Pass scheduling, semantic queries, product dependencies, and mutation contracts now live under `compiler/pipeline`, with contextual compilation services under `compiler/session` and typed graph domains under `compiler/ir`. Analysis implementations live under `compiler/analysis`; application preparation lives under `jaclang/build`. Internal consumers must migrate from the removed `compiler/driver`, `compiler/types`, `compiler/placement`, and `frontend/unitree` APIs. Generated artifacts are stored separately from syntax nodes. The JIR format version changes, invalidating older compiler caches.
