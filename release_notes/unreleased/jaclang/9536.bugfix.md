- **Fix: Typed graph hops no longer cost the whole node's degree**: `[node->:Edge:->]`, `visit [here->:Edge:->]` and edge removal used to scan every edge of the origin node (and, for removal, of both endpoints), so on a hub owning thousands of edges of other types each hop or `del` cost the hub's full degree. Nodes with more than a few edges now index their edges by type, keeping connect order and subtype matches, and unlinking an edge is constant time. Small hop results are also deduplicated without allocating a set. Traversal results and their order are unchanged.
