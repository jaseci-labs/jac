- **Bring-your-own-backend deploys via `[scale.database]` and a custom auth mode**: each backing service is declared as a `[scale.database.<name>]` table with `type`, `source`, and `url`. `source = "external"` wires your own Postgres/Redis/etc. (the `url` reads from `${ENV}` and ships through the app's Kubernetes Secret) and provisions nothing; `source = "managed"` keeps the in-cluster mongo/redis; `source = "disabled"` turns one off. Declaring any backend makes that list the full inventory, so an app that brings its own state stops getting the unused mongo/redis it never connected to. `[scale.auth].type = "custom"` leaves the built-in `/user/*`, SSO, and api-key endpoints unmounted for apps that authenticate themselves (e.g. Supabase), injecting `JAC_AUTH_*` env from the declared `issuer`/`jwks_url`/`audience`. Zero-config deploys and the legacy `mongodb_enabled`/`redis_enabled` flags behave exactly as before.
