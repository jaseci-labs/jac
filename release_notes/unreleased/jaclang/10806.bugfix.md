- **Fix: a failed or interrupted bundle seed no longer leaves `<app>-bundle-loader` behind** (#10785): `jac scale` waited for the bundle loader pod to start before the block that deletes it, so a loader that never ran (an image that can't be pulled, a storage class that can't bind, a `ReadWriteOnce` volume held on another node), the 180 s timeout, or a Ctrl-C during the wait left the pod in the namespace. The loader is now deleted on every one of those paths, and an upload that fails partway removes its own `.part` staging file from the bundle volume.
