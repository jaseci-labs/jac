- **Feature: Granular native demotion**: A native capability violation now demotes its smallest owning unit instead of refusing whole-module native codegen. An archetype method, an event ability, or a module-level free function that owns a violation becomes a per-callable seam (coverage-recorded with a reason naming the blocking construct, shipped as an abort stub when sealed, waiver-eligible through `seal_demotion_report`) while every sibling still lowers; free functions also gain the codegen-time error isolation methods already had. Violations with no owning callable (module-level imports, glob initializers, `init`/`postinit` bodies) stay module-fatal with a diagnostic that says why no seam could absorb them, and a native call site that targets a demoted callable is a structured E5092, never a silent bind to its abort stub.
