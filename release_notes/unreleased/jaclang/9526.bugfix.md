- **Fix: native string ops count codepoints, not bytes**: `len`, `ord`, indexing, slicing, `for`-iteration, `find`/`rfind`/`count`/`index`/`rindex`, `startswith`/`endswith`, and `ljust`/`rjust`/`center`/`zfill` all operated on UTF-8 bytes, so non-ASCII text produced wrong results, and `find` on a miss or `endswith` with an over-long suffix could read out of bounds.
