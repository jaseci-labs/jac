- **`save(obj, owner=...)` and `Jac.owner_of`**: `save` can create an anchor owned by a root other than the caller's, such as a minted org root, when the caller holds `WRITE` on it (`PermissionError` otherwise, `ValueError` for a missing or non-root owner or a re-own). `Jac.owner_of(obj)` returns the owning root. Omitting `owner` is unchanged.
