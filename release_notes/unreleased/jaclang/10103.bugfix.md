- **Fix: an app page at `/jobs` or under `/admin` loads on a direct visit or a refresh**: a file-based page such as `pages/jobs.jac` or `pages/admin/quotes.jac` rendered when reached by navigating inside the app, but loading the URL directly returned the scheduler API's 401 or the admin portal instead, because those platform routes were registered ahead of the SPA fallback. The platform's navigable routes now step aside when an app page explicitly claims the URL, and `jac run --dev` proxies a page under `/admin` to the dev server instead of the API. `POST`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on `/jobs`, the rest of `/admin`, and a root catch-all page are unaffected.
