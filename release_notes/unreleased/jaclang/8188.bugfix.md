- **Client pages importing a since-demoted module no longer die at mount with unimported runtime hooks**: when placement invalidated a module's ES output (demoting a client-pulled module, or pull-stamp changes), only the module-level `gen.js`/`gen.es_ast` caches were cleared; the per-node lowering fragments survived, and `EsastGenPass.enter_node` prunes any subtree whose `es_ast` is already cached. The re-lower therefore stitched the stale first-run fragments back together and skipped the lowering side effects that only fire on a real pass, in particular `_inject_runtime_import`, so a regenerated page referenced `useJacState`/`useState`/`useEffect` in its body with no `@jac/runtime` import lines and threw `ReferenceError` at mount behind the error boundary. All three invalidation sites in the placement solver now go through `_invalidate_module_es`, which walks the module tree and wipes every node-level ES cache together with the module-level ones, so re-lowering actually re-runs and re-registers its injected imports.
