- **Native `pathlib`, `fnmatch` and `logging`, plus the `os.path` gaps**: the native pathway gains a bundled `pathlib.Path` over the existing `os.path` floor (`Path(str)`/`Path(Path)`, `str()` and f-string interpolation, truthiness, `.name`, `.stem`, `.parent`, `/`, `.resolve()`, `.exists()`, `.is_dir()`), which is what `compiler/type_system/types.jac` needed to lower at all, along with a `fnmatch`/`fnmatchcase` glob matcher and a stderr `logging` surface. `os.sep` and its sibling module attributes now resolve, `os.path.getmtime` and `os.path.normcase` are reachable through the flat `import os`, and the bundled `os/path.jac` gains `relpath` and `normcase`. Each shim is pinned by a test that runs one fixture through both the sv and na pathways and requires identical output, so CPython is the oracle; a member outside a shim's provided surface fails the native compile naming that member rather than silently answering wrong.
