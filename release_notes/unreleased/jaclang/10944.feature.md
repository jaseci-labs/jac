- **Jac Scale: Prometheus, Loki and Tempo can keep their data on a volume**: each ran as one pod on an `emptyDir`, so a node drain, a lost node or a rollout replaced the pod and emptied it. `[scale.monitoring] storage_enabled = true` puts the three data directories on PersistentVolumeClaims (`storage_class`, `prometheus_storage`, `loki_storage`, `tempo_storage`), rolls them with `Recreate`, caps Prometheus at 80% of its volume, and gives Loki a retention period (`loki_retention`, default `168h`; it had none). Off by default, and with it off the monitoring pods are deployed exactly as before. Turning it on replaces each of the three pods once, and `jac scale destroy` deletes the claims. See [Monitoring data storage](../../reference/plugins/jac-scale-kubernetes.md#monitoring-data-storage).
