- **Jac Scale: `jac scale status`, `resource_status` and `scale` see a deployed app**: they looked for a Deployment named after the app, which no deploy creates, so status always showed the app as not deployed or unknown and scaling failed. Status now reports the whole fleet, the least healthy service setting the verdict, and `scale` resizes one service, the app itself by default or the `[apps]` entry named by the new `service` argument.
