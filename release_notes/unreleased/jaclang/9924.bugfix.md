- **Fix: native macOS binaries read 16-byte constants from where they are**: When the Mach-O linker merged a unit's section into one of the same name, the merged section kept the alignment of whichever unit contributed first, so a 16-byte constant merged behind an 8-byte-aligned one could land 8 bytes off a 16-byte address. arm64's `ldr q` cannot express that offset and read the bytes ahead of the constant, which made vectorized code (a native `int.from_bytes`, for one) return garbage in some builds and not in others. A merged section now takes its strictest part's alignment, common symbols are allocated on their own alignment, and the image's `__text`, `__const` and `__data` start on the strictest alignment of their contents instead of a fixed 16 bytes.
