- **Fix: native code handles dicts and sets keyed on `any`, containers of `None`, and `int()` of bytes**: `dict[any, ...]`, `set[any]`, and dict literals and comprehensions mixing key types now lower natively, with keys hashed and compared by value the way CPython does (`1`, `1.0` and `True` are one key, as are `0.0` and `-0.0`). A dict or list whose values are always `None` no longer crashes the native backend. `int(b"200")` parses its text instead of crashing.
