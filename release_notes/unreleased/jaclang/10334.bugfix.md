- **Fix: f-strings release the strings made for their pieces**: in native code, a piece whose value is a string made only for the f-string, such as `{w[1:]}`, `{w.upper()}` or `{a + b}`, was copied into the result and never released, leaking about 63 bytes per piece. Three million such f-strings reached 183 MB of peak RSS, and now stay under 1 MB.
