- **Fix: a `match` capture that rebinds a local is checked against its type**: after `title = Label()`, `case str() as title` or a bare `case title` could put a `str` or the whole subject into `title`, and `jac check` passed while the run failed on `title.text`. A capture is now held to the local's type like any other rebind and reported as E1001. An `as` capture binds what its pattern matches; a bare capture binds the subject without what earlier unguarded cases matched.
