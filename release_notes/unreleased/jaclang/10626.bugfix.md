- **Fix: KEDA autoscaling works on any target length**: KEDA refuses a ScaledObject whose name or HPA name is over 63 characters, so `[*.http_activation]` failed with a 403 on a target of 46 characters or more, and the metric autoscaler failed on an app of 42 or more. jac-scale now gives those a short hashed name, and shortens the `app` label on a target over 63 characters. A name KEDA already accepts never changes, so no live ScaledObject is renamed. This removes the 46-character limit the HPA name fix in #10598 left in place.
