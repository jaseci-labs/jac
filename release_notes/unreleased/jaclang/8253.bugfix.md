- **Fix: a variadic parameter of a generic archetype's erased type parameter**: `def get(*fallback: T)` on a generic archetype lowered its pack slot to the erased element pointer rather than to a list, so the call site silently rebound the arguments positionally and the callee read the pack as a string, crashing on the first call that passed one. The pack now lays out as the pointer-flavoured native list, a by-value type argument is refused at the construction site the way an erased field's is, and a callee whose pack slot did not lay out demotes its caller instead of binding into it.
- **Fix: inherited fields through a consumer subclass of an imported archetype**: A module that subclassed an archetype declared elsewhere laid its subclass out with only its own fields, so every inherited read, write and constructor keyword refused or was dropped. The subclass now adopts its imported ancestor's slot assignment, so both units agree on where a field lives; where a vtable-less ancestor leaves no assignment that satisfies both, the access stays refused and the diagnostic names the field and the ancestor that declares it.
