- **Native: optional containers keep their element layout**: a `T | None` slot lowers to a presence tag beside the container pointer, and every loop, comprehension, slice and index over one used to hand that wrapper to the container helpers, crashing the emitter and costing the whole callable its lowering. The payload is now unwrapped at each consuming head, and an absent optional raises the same `TypeError` CPython raises rather than reading a null. `x or []` and `x or {}` take their element layout from the operand beside them instead of guessing and erasing both branches to an untyped pointer, and a null container is falsy rather than a dereference of its length field. Two emitter crashes that resist a root fix are now named refusals instead: a call argument that never reached the callee's parameter type names the callee and position, and a builtin-method emitter that cannot take its operands names the method.
