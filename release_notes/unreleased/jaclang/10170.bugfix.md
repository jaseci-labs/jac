- **Fix: `bytes` and `bytearray` over a range or tuple lower natively**: `bytes(range(256))`, `bytes((7, 8))` and `bytearray(range(n))` were refused by the native backend (E5092) and their function sent to Python; the constructors now drain any iterable of ints through the list path, as `set` does. A conversion that raises `ValueError` (an int past 255) no longer leaks: the drained list, the iterable and a `bytearray`'s fresh buffer are released on the error path.
