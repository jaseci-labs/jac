- **Fix: native bool arithmetic renders as int**: On the native backend an f-string over bool arithmetic (`f"{(x > 1) + (x > 0)}"`) or over a sized integer (`i32`, `u8`, `i16`, ...) rendered as an empty string, `-b`, `+b`, `~b` and `abs(b)` rendered `True`/`False`, `b & c` printed `0`/`1`, and `s = b + b; print(s)` printed `True`. Bool arithmetic now yields `int` as in Python, `&`, `|` and `^` between two bools stay `bool`, and f-strings format integers of every width. The checker now types `b + b`, `b << n`, `-b` and `~b` as `int` instead of `bool`.
