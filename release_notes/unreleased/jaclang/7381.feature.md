- **Feature: `Final` and `readonly` immutability is enforced**: `jac check` now rejects writes to an immutable binding with a new `E1135` diagnostic. A `typing.Final` local, `has` field, glob, or imported name errors on every form of write: plain, multi-target, and augmented assignment, tuple unpacking including starred elements, walrus, `del`, and re-using the name as a `for` loop variable, a `with ... as` alias, an `except ... as` alias, or a `match` capture pattern. Those four rebinding forms bind a fresh local when they appear in a nested function, so only a rebinding in the scope that owns the binding is a write. `readonly` members of npm `.d.ts` interfaces are write-protected the same way, and an imported `Final` keeps its qualifier through the module interface cache, so a cached rebuild enforces it exactly as a cold one does. The declaring assignment, constructor initialization, and `init`/`postinit` assignment of a no-default `Final` field in the object that declares it stay legal, matching Pyright.
