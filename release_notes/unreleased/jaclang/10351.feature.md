- **`impls-in-annex` keeps every impl in an annex inside an impl folder**: the new lint rule (W3056, on under `select = ["all"]`, off under `["default"]`) flags an `impl` written in its module and an `.impl.jac` annex sitting beside its module. `jac fmt --lintfix` and `jac check --lint --fix` move the impls into `impl/<module>.impl.jac` (or `<module>.impl/` for a dotted stem such as a platform variant) and relocate a sibling annex there.
