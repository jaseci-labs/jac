- **Performance: native `sort(key=...)` and `sorted(..., key=...)` no longer take quadratic time**: a key sort was a bubble sort that called the key function twice per comparison. Each key is now computed once, in element order, and the positions are merge-sorted by those keys. The sort stays stable, `reverse=True` included. Sorting 40 000 ints by a key took 1.25 s and now takes under 10 ms, and a million take 0.05 s.
