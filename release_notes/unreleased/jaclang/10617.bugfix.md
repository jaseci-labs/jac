- **Fix: two sign-ups racing for the same username or email no longer both succeed**: The second one used to get a 201 and a token for an account that listed the identity, while logging in with it reached the first account. It now gets `IDENTITY_TAKEN` and no account is created. A username or email held by a disabled account is now also reported as taken at sign-up, instead of creating an account that could not log in with it.
