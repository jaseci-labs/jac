- **Fix: a workload scaled to zero on purpose is `inactive`, not `failed`, and every status reader agrees**: `jac scale status`, `ScaleClient.resource_status`, the fleet-ready gate at the end of `jac scale deploy`, and the Ops Console's `/admin/ops/deploy` each did their own replica arithmetic on the Deployment, so a healthy app KEDA had idled to zero read `FAILED` in one place and `Pending` in another, a waking cold start read `FAILED` for its whole boot, a rollout past its progress deadline still read healthy while the old replica served, and a METRIC-mode scale-to-zero service timed out the deploy's readiness gate outright because only HTTP activation was allowed to sit at zero. One verdict, `assess_workload` in `jaclang.scale.models.resource_status`, now decides for all four, from the Deployment's rollout observation and availability plus the scaling intent `jac scale deploy` stamps on it as the `jac-scale.replica-floor` annotation. `ResourceStatus` is `inactive`, `activating`, `active`, `deactivating`, `degraded` or `unknown` (replacing `pending`, `running`, `failed` and the never-produced `stopped`), `ResourceStatusInfo` gains `available_replicas`, `updated_replicas`, `replica_floor` and `reason`, `/admin/ops/deploy` reports that status in place of `complete`/`progressing`/`stuck`, and a redeploy no longer resets `spec.replicas` on any autoscaled Deployment, not only HTTP-activated ones. Fixes #7405.
