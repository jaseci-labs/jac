- **Fix: a tuple returned by a call and indexed at once is released**: in native code, `pair(i)[1]` (and `struct.unpack(fmt, data)[0]`) read the field and never released the tuple. Every such read leaked the tuple and what it held: 128 bytes per call with an object inside, 192 with a string. Three million calls reached 366 MB of peak RSS, and now stay under 1 MB.
