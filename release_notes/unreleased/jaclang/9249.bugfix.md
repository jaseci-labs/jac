- A server refuses to start, naming every offending target, when an `@schedule` cannot run as written: no timing or more than one kind of it, an `interval` that is not a positive number of seconds, a `cron` field out of range or in syntax the engine does not support, a day that never occurs in the listed months, a malformed `date`, or timing on a `DYNAMIC` target. Before, `interval=0` re-fired in a busy loop, a negative interval starved every other task, a spec with no timing was silently dropped, and `cron="60 * * * *"` crashed boot after the routes were registered. The cron engine now jumps field by field instead of scanning a year of minutes, so a valid rare schedule such as `0 0 29 2 *` no longer crashes boot when the next February 29 is more than a year away. A `date` that has already passed logs a warning naming the task.
