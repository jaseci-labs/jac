- **Runtime: `__jac_access_for__(caller)`, a caller-aware access policy that ownership does not pre-empt**: `__jac_access__` is called with no arguments and runs after the owner fast-path, so an archetype could fix a level for every non-owner but never decide per caller, and never at all for anchors the caller's root owns; per-caller roles were therefore only expressible as endpoint checks. An archetype may now declare `__jac_access_for__(caller: UUID)`, which is consulted before the owner fast-path: ownership admits and the role decides, so even the owning root can be held to `READ`. Returning `None` falls through to ownership and stored grants; the system root keeps its bypass. Archetypes declaring only the zero-arg hook, or neither, resolve exactly as before, and when both are declared the caller-aware hook wins. Documented in the `jac-sv-multi-user` skill. Fixes #8459.
