- **Fix: a sealed image never carries `.env` files or key material, and honors `.jacignore`**: `build_jab` (desktop builds, `jac build`) copied the whole project root, so a project's `.env` and its secrets shipped inside the `.jab`. The seal now uses the same never-ship floor as the deploy bundle (`.env`, `.env.*`, private keys and certificates) and leaves out what `.jacignore` parks.
