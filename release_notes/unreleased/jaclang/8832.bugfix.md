- **Fix: a program nested too deeply to compile says where**: A very long operator chain or a deeply nested expression used to fail with an internal "maximum recursion depth exceeded" message and no location, and very deep bracket nesting could crash `jac` outright. It now reports `E0111` at the line and column of the offending expression, with a hint to split it up. Expressions can nest up to 200 brackets or prefix operators deep. A program that recurses while it runs still reports its own failure with its traceback.
