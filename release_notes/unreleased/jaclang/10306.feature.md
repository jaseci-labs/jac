- **ModelPool can stay on the member that answered**: `ModelPool(strategy="fallback", sticky=True)` starts the rest of a `by` call at the member that answered after a failure, so an agent turn waits on a failed provider once instead of on every model call. Every switch is also reported, as a `model_fallback` stream event (`from`, `to`, `error`) and under `fallbacks` on the usage record of the call that answered.
