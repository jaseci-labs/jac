- **Fix: one unreachable service no longer fails gateway calls it does not own**: when the gateway probes services for a walker or other builtin path, a service whose connection is refused (restarting, scaling, a dead pod) is now skipped and the next service is tried, instead of the whole call failing with `503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` naming that service. A request that reached a service and was then lost, or timed out, still fails fast rather than being replayed elsewhere.
