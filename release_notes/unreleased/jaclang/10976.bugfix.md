- **Fix: the gateway stops forwarding a deleted account's session token**: Session tokens now carry an `iss` claim naming the identity store that holds the account: `__gateway__` for the fleet's store, or the app's name for an app with a store of its own. The gateway removes the bearer and session cookie of a token of its own store for an account it no longer holds, over HTTP and WebSockets, instead of forwarding it to apps that would serve it until it expires. An app with its own store provisions only sessions of the fleet's store. Tokens minted before this change keep today's behaviour until they expire.
