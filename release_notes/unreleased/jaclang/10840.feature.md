- **neojac compatibility: graph spaces, stdjac, and the semantics diffwalker needs**: `archwire.jac` takes a `graph spaces` block that declares a project's spaces (browser, hermes, native hosts with entry modules and module patterns) and its apps (`run`, `serve`), deriving each app's kind, entry point and placement pins; `jac fmt` keeps its layout. The `stdjac` library (`remote`, `graph`, `codec`, `plan`, `by`, `llm`) is importable by its short name and runs on jac's runtime, with `by llm(conversation=, tools=, parallelize=)` and `Iterator[ByEvent]` streamed answers on byllm. A page function's parameters become URL segments and page props, a client app takes its packages and Vite settings from its `[npm.<tree>]` and `eco/<tree>` tree, an `Iterator[T]` endpoint streams typed items to the browser through `__jacCallStream`, and the browser runtime exposes the remote-error family, arrangements and local users. The checker types `flow`/`wait` as `Task[T]`, accepts a reserved word as a keyword-argument name, narrows across a `with` block, and reports an attribute read on a value that may be `None` as W1099 (a warning, no longer E1099).
