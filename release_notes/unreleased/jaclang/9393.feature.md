- **Feature: Native `time` module**: `import time` on the native pathway now binds the bundled `na_stdlib/time.jac` Mechanism-F surface over a per-OS libc FFI floor (`clock_gettime`/`clock_settime`/`clock_nanosleep` on Linux, `nanosleep` on Darwin), replacing the compiler intercept. The surface adds `process_time`, `thread_time`, `clock_gettime_ns`, `clock_settime_ns`, and the `CLOCK_*` constants on top of the previous `time`/`monotonic`/`perf_counter`/`*_ns`/`sleep` set; `sleep` uses an absolute monotonic deadline with `EINTR` retry and matches CPython's error behavior for negative, NaN, and out-of-range inputs; clock failures raise errno-mapped `OSError` subclasses (`PermissionError`, ...).
