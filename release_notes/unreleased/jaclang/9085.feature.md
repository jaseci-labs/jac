- **Feature: one observability collector per cluster instead of one per app**: node-exporter, Alloy, Prometheus and kube-state-metrics now deploy once into a shared `jac-observability` namespace and serve every app on the cluster, replacing the per-app copies each deploy used to stamp out. The two DaemonSets stop scaling with app count (`apps x nodes` pods becomes `nodes` pods), and the three cluster-scoped ClusterRoles they need drop from `3 x apps` to 3. Per-app metric attribution is unchanged: each app gets its own scrape job, its own scrape credential, and `namespace`/`jac_app` labels on its series, and each app's logs are namespace-filtered before they reach that app's own Loki. Grafana, Loki and Tempo stay per-app. Set `[scale.kubernetes] observability_namespace` to move the shared tier.
