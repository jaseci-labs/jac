- **Fix: Native container equality is structural**: On the native backend, `==` and `!=` now compare dictionaries by their key/value pairs (insertion order does not matter) instead of by address. Lists, tuples, dicts and sets nested inside other containers compare by content, including `in` over lists and tuples of containers. `keys()` and `items()` views compare as sets and `values()` compares by identity. Objects honour a user `__eq__` / `__ne__`, including Python's rule that a subclass operand on the right is asked first.
