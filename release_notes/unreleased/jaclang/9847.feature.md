- **Feature: emailers send Reply-To and attachments**: `Emailer.send_email` takes optional `reply_to` and `attachments` (`EmailAttachment`: filename, bytes, MIME type, optional `content_id`). The SMTP emailer sets `Reply-To`, shows files with a `content_id` inside the HTML body as `cid:<content_id>` (inline images), and attaches the rest. Sends without them are unchanged. An attachment's `content_id` may be given with or without angle brackets, and a `mime_type` that isn't `type/subtype` is sent as `application/octet-stream`. A header value with a line break (recipient, subject, `reply_to` or attachment filename) makes the SMTP emailer's `send_email` return `False` rather than raise.
