- **Fix: scale-to-zero KEDA services deploy and report status correctly**: A microservice configured with `autoscaler_engine = "keda"`, `hpa.min = 0`, and a real trigger previously failed `jac scale deploy --dry-run` outright, could hang a deploy for up to 20 minutes waiting on a service that was never meant to start, and reported `FAILED` instead of `STOPPED` from `get_status`. One shared predicate now decides scale-to-zero eligibility everywhere, so a legitimately idle service is recognized as such across dry-run validation, the rollout wait, and live status checks.
