- **Fix: a service that leaves scale-to-zero while idle comes back up**: turning `[scale.kubernetes.http_activation]` off and redeploying while the service sat at 0 replicas deleted the KEDA objects but left the Deployment at 0, which the HPA never scales up from, so the deploy timed out and the service stayed down. The deploy now sets such a Deployment to its replica floor once the KEDA objects are gone; a running service and a KEDA `idle_replicas = 0` service are left alone.
