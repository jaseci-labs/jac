- **Fix: the sv-to-sv circuit breaker now trips after the number of failures it advertises**: a failed service-to-service call was counted as a single breaker failure no matter how many times it had retried on the wire, so the breaker needed roughly fifteen failed round trips to open instead of the five its threshold names. Failures are now counted per round trip, and a call stops retrying as soon as the breaker opens, so a recovery probe costs one round trip instead of three. This makes callers shed load from an unreachable dependency sooner: five consecutive failed round trips now open the breaker for the cooldown period, and any successful round trip resets the count to zero.
