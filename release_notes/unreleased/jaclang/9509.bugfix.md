- **Fix: a `nogc` move after a borrow no longer double-frees**: Under `--memory nogc`, moving an owned local after it had been borrowed (`f(&mut a); m = a;`) left both bindings owning the payload, so it was freed at the source's last use while the new owner still used it. The move now hands the payload over wherever the ownership checker proves it, including after `&`/`&mut` arguments, borrows in loops, field borrows, `own` parameters and optional `own` locals.
