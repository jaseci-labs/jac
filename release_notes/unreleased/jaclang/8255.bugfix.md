- **Native: `open(path, encoding=...)` refuses instead of dropping the keyword**: the emitter read `open` positionally and ignored everything past the mode, so a declared `encoding` would have compiled to a `fopen` that silently ignored it. It now demotes the calling function to a Python-only seam, and `jac0core/helpers.jac` (which asks for an encoding and sits under most of the front end) reaches the backend instead of refusing as a whole module.
