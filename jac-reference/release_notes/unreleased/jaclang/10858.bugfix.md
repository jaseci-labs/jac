- **Fix: a fleet app on the gateway's identity store no longer recreates accounts it does not know**: a fleet app now provisions the users of gateway tokens only when its own env sets `JAC_DB_URL`, so it holds an identity store of its own; the local orchestrator and the Kubernetes builder make that call with one helper and tell the app. An app on the shared store, the default, refuses a deleted or invented account with 401 instead of writing a blank record into the gateway's accounts, which brought deleted accounts back and let their tokens renew. Local fleet apps no longer provision, and `/user/refresh-token` and `/user/logout-all` now work on apps that share the store. A separate store the launcher cannot see fails with 401 until that app's own env sets `JAC_DB_URL`.
